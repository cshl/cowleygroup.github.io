The homeworks will be interactive groups where each person takes a turn at a different role:

  • student (at whiteboard): present topic, answer questions
  • scribe: take notes on PI-student interaction, what went well? what could have gone better?

Below are the assigned groups and topics for each Homework. After the first PI-student-scribe, you will all shift by one for another 20min. The final 20min will be one more shift. Each person will have a chance for each role.

Scribes will turn in their notes (1 paragraph) by emailing Ben (cowley at cshl) a one page pdf by the deadline.
